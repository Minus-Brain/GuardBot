GuardBot is a powerful Telegram moderation bot that protects your groups from spam, floods, and raids automatically - with a quick 3-minute setup.

**🔗 Official website: [https://guardbot.tech](https://guardbot.tech)**

## Features
* **Anti-Spam & Flood Control** - Automatically detects and deletes spam messages, links, and restricts flooding users.
* **Bot & Raid Protection** - Prevents mass bot additions and malicious user raids in real-time.
* **Easy 3-Minute Setup** - Add the bot to your group, grant admin rights, and it instantly starts working.
* **24/7 Continuous Moderation** - Keeps your community clean around the clock without human intervention.

## How to Start
1. Visit our official website: [guardbot.tech](https://guardbot.tech)
2. Click **Start** to open the bot in Telegram.
3. Add **@ForGuardBot** to your group and grant it Administrator permissions (Delete messages, Ban users).
4. Enjoy a clean and safe chat!

## Tech & Architecture
Developed using **Python** with a focus on speed, reliability, and secure interaction with the Telegram API. Designed to handle high-load environments and large communities smoothly.
